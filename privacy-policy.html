The short version: we collect only what you choose to send us, we use it to respond to you and to prepare your documents, we do not sell or rent your information, and you can ask us at any time to see, correct, or delete it.

This Privacy Policy explains how The Juris Institute (“The Juris Institute,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you visit thejurisinstitute.com (the “Website”) or use our legal document preparation, legal education, and coaching services (the “Services”). By using the Website or the Services, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. Please also review our Terms of Service.

1. Information We Collect

Information you give us

We collect information only when you choose to provide it, including:

  • Inquiry form information: your name, email address, phone number (optional), state, the type of matter, your timeframe, how you heard about us, and the description you write about your situation.
  • Project information: if you engage our Services, the facts, documents, records, and instructions you send us so we can prepare your documents or provide coaching. Depending on your matter, this may include information about your property, family, finances, business, health care wishes, or a pending court case.
  • Communications: emails and messages you send us, and our replies.
  • Payment information: when you pay a fee, your payment is handled by a third-party payment processor. We receive confirmation of payment and limited details such as your name, the amount, and the date. We do not receive or store your full card or bank account number.

Please do not send us Social Security numbers, full financial account numbers, or other highly sensitive identifiers unless a specific document requires them and we have asked for them.

Information collected automatically

We do not use advertising trackers, analytics scripts, or tracking pixels on the Website. Like most websites, however, certain technical information is processed automatically by the service providers that deliver the Website to you:

  • Hosting and security: our hosting and content delivery provider, Cloudflare, processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device information, and the pages requested, in order to deliver the Website and protect it against attacks and abuse. Cloudflare may set strictly necessary security cookies for this purpose.
  • Fonts: the Website loads typefaces from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and browser information when the fonts load.
  • Embedded videos: our blog articles include videos from our YouTube channel, embedded using YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode. YouTube does not set tracking cookies when the page loads, but if you play a video, YouTube (a Google service) may collect information under Google’s own privacy policy.

Free tools and resources

Our free calculators and checklists run entirely in your web browser. The numbers and answers you enter into them are not sent to us or stored by us.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • respond to your inquiry and send you a written flat-fee quote;
  • prepare your documents, provide legal education and coaching, and communicate with you about your project;
  • process payments and keep business, accounting, and tax records;
  • keep the Website secure, prevent spam and fraud, and fix technical problems;
  • improve our Services and our educational content; and
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not add you to a marketing email list unless you ask to be added, and we do not use your information for targeted advertising.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing or for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only in these limited situations:

  • Service providers that help us operate, under obligations to protect your information and use it only to provide their services to us. These include Web3Forms, which delivers inquiry form submissions to our email inbox; our email provider; our payment processor; and Cloudflare, which hosts the Website.
  • At your direction. We share information with others only when you ask us to. We do not communicate with third parties about your matter, such as your accountant, the other party, or a court, on your behalf. You decide who receives the documents we prepare for you.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information when we believe in good faith that the law requires it, such as in response to a subpoena or court order, or to protect our rights, our clients, or the public.
  • Business transfer. If our business is sold, merged, or reorganized, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Privacy Policy.

4. Third-Party Websites and Donations

The Website links to websites we do not control, including YouTube, court and government websites, and PayPal. Our “Donate Now” button takes you to a charitable fundraiser hosted on PayPal’s platform. Donations are processed by PayPal and its charitable partners, not by us, and we do not receive your payment details. Their own privacy policies govern any information you give them.

5. Cookies and “Do Not Track”

We do not set advertising or analytics cookies. The only cookies associated with the Website are strictly necessary security cookies that Cloudflare may set, and cookies YouTube may set if you choose to play an embedded video. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Some browsers send a “Do Not Track” signal. Because we do not track visitors over time or across other websites, we do not change our practices in response to these signals. We also do not allow third parties to collect information about your online activity on our Website for their own advertising purposes.

6. How Long We Keep Information

We keep inquiry information for as long as needed to respond to you and follow up on your request. If you become a client, we keep your project information and communications for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, answer later questions about your documents, maintain accurate business and tax records, and meet our legal obligations. After that, we delete or securely dispose of the information.

7. How We Protect Information

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. These include encrypted (HTTPS) connections to the Website, access controls on our accounts, and limiting access to people who need it to serve you. No method of transmitting or storing data is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Because email is not a fully secure channel, please ask us about a more secure way to send highly sensitive documents.

Because The Juris Institute is not a law firm and does not form attorney-client relationships, your communications with us are not protected by attorney-client privilege. We treat your information as confidential as described in this Privacy Policy, but we may be legally required to disclose it, for example under a valid subpoena.

8. Your Choices and Rights

You may ask us at any time to:

  • tell you what personal information we have about you;
  • correct or update information that is inaccurate;
  • delete your information, subject to records we must keep for legal, tax, or accounting purposes; or
  • stop sending you any non-essential communications.

To make a request, email us at info@TheJurisInstitute.com with the subject line “Privacy Request.” We may ask you to confirm your identity before we act, and we will respond within 45 days. You will not be treated differently for making a request.

Depending on where you live, your state’s laws may give you additional rights. We will honor those rights as required by applicable law.

9. Children’s Privacy

The Website and Services are intended for adults. They are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it. When a project involves information about a minor, such as naming a guardian in a will, that information is provided by the adult client and handled as project information under this Privacy Policy.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the updated version on this page and change the “Last updated” date at the top. If we make a material change to how we use or share personal information, we will display a notice on the Website before the change takes effect. Your continued use of the Website or Services after an update means you acknowledge the updated Privacy Policy.
