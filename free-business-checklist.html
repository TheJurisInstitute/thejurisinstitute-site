The LLC & Contract
Checklist for Business Owners

12 steps to protect your business when you form an LLC and before you sign any contract, plus three habits that keep you protected.

The Juris Institute — 25+ Years of Legal Experience

Most business legal problems start small: a missing operating agreement, a contract signed in your own name, a vague scope of work, a missed annual report. This free checklist walks you through the 12 steps that prevent them.

What’s Inside

  1. Choose your state of formation on purpose
  2. Confirm your business name is available, and protect it
  3. Appoint a reliable registered agent
  4. Sign an operating agreement, even for a one-member LLC
  5. Get an EIN and open a separate business bank account
  6. Calendar your annual report, licenses and tax deadlines
  7. Make sure the right parties are named, and sign as the LLC
  8. Pin down the scope of work and the deliverables
  9. Spell out payment terms and what happens if someone pays late
  10. Read the termination, liability and indemnification clauses twice
  11. Check who owns the work and who must keep things confidential
  12. Know where and how disputes will be resolved

Plus three habits that keep your business protected.
