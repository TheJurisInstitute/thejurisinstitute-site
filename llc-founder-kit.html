Questions

Does the kit file my LLC with the state?

No. You file your Articles of Organization (some states call them a Certificate of Formation) yourself, using your state’s own form. The guide and checklist walk you through it.

Will the templates work in my state?

The templates are written to work under most states’ LLC laws, with blanks for your state and drafting notes where states commonly differ. LLC laws and requirements vary, so check your state’s current rules.

How does the upgrade credit work?

Within 60 days of buying the kit, request a quote for custom operating agreement drafting and include your PayPal receipt number. The full $149 you paid for the kit is applied to the flat fee. One credit per kit purchase.

When should I have an agreement custom drafted instead?

Consider custom drafting if owners contribute unequally or contribute work instead of money, if you have outside investors or a manager who is not an owner, if profits will be shared differently from ownership, if you plan an S corporation election, or if owners live in different states.

Will you fill in or review my documents?

Not as part of the kit. If you want your documents prepared for you, use your upgrade credit toward our drafting service.

What format are the files?

Editable Microsoft Word documents, delivered as a zip file download. You can fill them in on your computer or print them and fill them in by hand.
