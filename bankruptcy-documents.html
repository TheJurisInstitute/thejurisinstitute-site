Who We Serve

Who Needs Bankruptcy Document Preparation?

Individuals and small business owners considering or pursuing bankruptcy relief.

Individuals Considering Chapter 7 Discharge

Chapter 7 bankruptcy can discharge most unsecured debts — credit cards, medical bills, personal loans — in a matter of months. A complete, accurate petition package is essential to a successful filing.

Individuals Pursuing Chapter 13 Reorganization

Chapter 13 allows you to repay arrears on a secured mortgage, catch up on car payments, and restructure your debts over a three-to-five-year plan. We prepare the full petition and plan documents.

Small Business Owners

Individual business owners whose personal and business finances are intertwined often consider personal bankruptcy to address both. We prepare Chapter 7 and Chapter 13 documents for individuals with business-related debts.

Anyone Who Has Been Through Dismissal

If your prior bankruptcy case was dismissed — often due to incomplete or improperly prepared documents — we prepare a complete and accurate package to support a successful refiling.
