How accurate is the court deadline calculator?

The court deadline calculator is designed for general educational purposes and as a starting point for organizing your timeline. Court deadline rules are complex, jurisdiction-specific, and subject to local rules, standing orders, and recent amendments. Always confirm deadlines with the court's clerk, your state's rules of civil procedure, and applicable local rules. If you have a specific filing deadline, verify it independently — do not rely solely on any online calculator for compliance.

Can I save or print the results from the budget builder or debt calculator?

Yes. Once you have entered your information and generated your budget or debt payoff plan, you can print the results using your browser's print function (Ctrl+P or Command+P). The tools do not automatically save your data. If you close the browser or refresh the page, you will need to re-enter your information. For ongoing tracking, consider using the printed output as a reference while you work through your financial plan.

What is zero-based budgeting?

Zero-based budgeting is a method in which every dollar of your income is assigned a specific purpose — expenses, savings, debt repayment — so that your income minus your outgoing amounts equals zero. This does not mean you spend every dollar; it means every dollar has a job. The zero-based budget builder on this page walks you through this process step by step. This is the budgeting methodology taught by Dave Ramsey and used in our Financial Education Coaching service.

What is the debt snowball method?

The debt snowball is a debt elimination strategy developed by Dave Ramsey in which you list your debts from smallest balance to largest, make minimum payments on all debts, and direct every extra dollar at the smallest debt first. When that debt is paid off, you roll its minimum payment to the next-smallest debt — creating a growing 'snowball' of momentum. Research shows that this method works for most people not because it is mathematically optimal, but because the early wins create motivation to keep going. Our Financial Education Coaching uses this methodology.
