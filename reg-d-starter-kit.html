Questions

Will you fill in or review my documents?

No. The kit is self-help. You complete every document yourself. We can answer questions about the kit and its contents, but we cannot complete, review or advise on your offering.

Is a PPM legally required?

Not always, but it is common practice, and federal and state antifraud rules apply to every private offering whether or not a PPM is required. The guides explain when issuers usually prepare one.

Do I still need a lawyer?

We strongly recommend that a licensed securities attorney review your completed documents before you approach investors. The kit helps you arrive prepared, which can shorten that review.

What format are the files?

Editable Microsoft Word documents and one Excel spreadsheet, delivered as a zip file download.

Can I use the kit for more than one offering?

Yes, for offerings by your own business and its affiliates. You may not resell or share the blank templates, or use them to prepare documents for others as a paid service.
